Data Governance & Fair Play

1. Zero-Peeking Cryptographic Commitment

Before each turn, the AI generates a secret nonce and produces a SHA-256 cryptographic commitment of its intended action. The move is fixed in stone before you click, guaranteeing complete fair play.

2. Explicit Consent & Anonymity (GDPR/CCPA)

By default, all games run in pure ephemeral in-memory mode. No PII, cookies, or persistent identifiers are captured. If you opt in, only round action tokens (R, P, S) and timestamps are collected to analyze model convergence.

3. Right to Erasure

You may wipe all model observations and telemetry at any time by clicking "Wipe Session (Right to be Forgotten)".
